Your Blood files

The game files are not included. Pick them from your own copy of Blood (One Unit Whole Blood from GOG or Steam, Fresh Supply or the CD). They stay on this device, inside this web app — nothing is uploaded.

Needed: BLOOD.RFF, GUI.RFF, SOUNDS.RFF, SURFACE.DAT, TABLES.DAT, VOXEL.DAT, BLOOD.INI, TILES000.ART–TILES017.ART. Optional: the movie folder, blood02.ogg… (CD music), add-ons and mods.

Select all files of your Blood folder at once, or a .zip of it. On iPad: in the Files app, open the Blood folder, tap Select, Select All, then Open.

Settings, saves and files

Restore a backup by choosing its .zip with “Choose files”. Your saves stay in this browser on this device.

Install it

Online play: MULTIPLAYER > ONLINE. Leave SERVER empty to use the server this page comes from and enter its key. LAN games aren't possible in a browser.

Blood Extra Crispy: download the mod (by VGames, on ModDB) and choose its BEC_1.2 folder or its .zip with “Choose files”. Then MODS > BLOOD EXTRA CRISPY in the game: its look and sounds, and USE on a nearly dead enemy finishes it in first person.

Controls: keyboard and mouse (click the game to capture the mouse), a game controller, or the touch controls on tablets and phones.
