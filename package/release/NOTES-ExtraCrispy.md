# Blood Extra Crispy in NotBlood Chosen (Android test build)

A test of the *Blood Extra Crispy* mod (by VGames, for Nightdive's Refreshed Supply) as a mod for NotBlood Chosen. The normal release is [v1.2](https://github.com/AdrianPol-projects/NotBlood-Chosen/releases/latest); this build is only for trying the mod.

**The app:** it's called *NotBlood Crispy* and installs next to NotBlood instead of replacing it. Both use the same `NotBlood` folder on the phone, so your Blood files, settings and saves are shared.

**Setup:**
1. Install the APK. If you have never used NotBlood on this phone, copy your Blood files into the `NotBlood` folder first (see the [v1.2 notes](https://github.com/AdrianPol-projects/NotBlood-Chosen/releases/latest)).
2. Download **Blood Extra Crispy v1.2** from ModDB (`BEC_v1.2_RS.zip`) and unpack it.
3. Copy its `BEC_1.2` folder, unchanged, into `NotBlood/mods/` on the phone. The result is `NotBlood/mods/BEC_1.2/EXTRAC.RFF` and so on.
4. Start NotBlood Crispy, go to **MODS**, choose **BLOOD EXTRA CRISPY**. The game restarts with the mod.

**What you get:**
- **Its look:** the title and menu background, its edited sprites, and its **3D models** (voxels) for about 570 things: switches, levers, valves, bottles, signs, lamps, chandeliers, pickups, the Maxim gun and the cannons. OPTIONS > DISPLAY SETUP > VOXELS: **SOME** (the default: switches, levers, valves, pickups and the mod's own things), **ALL** (also its wall decorations and props, slower on phones) or **OFF**. A level loads only the ones it uses.
- **Its sounds.**
- **Its maps:** the mod's versions of the official levels, with its extra decorations, gore and enemies.
- **Gory Kills:** walk up to a nearly dead enemy, face it and press **USE**. Caleb finishes it in first person, heals a little, and kicks the body away. They work on cultists (all four), axe zombies, fat zombies, flesh gargoyles, gill beasts, innocents, hell hounds and phantasms, and on the mod's Crimson Innocents, Skeletal Zombies, crawling half zombies, zombie heads and attack dogs. The enemy must be badly hurt (below about 12 health), except innocents, heads, half zombies and dogs.
- **Puke on your face** (fat zombies, pods, and the red blood of Crimson Innocents) stays on the screen until you press **USE**: Caleb wipes it off with his hand.
- **Quick kick:** USE on an enemy that isn't hurt enough shows Caleb's foot and kicks it back. Burning enemies, beasts, Necromancers, Liches, Monster Dogs, Slime Zombies and the Inferno always get kicked. You can kick once a second.
- **In Bloodbath and team games** you can kick other players too, and finish off one who is nearly dead (it counts as your frag). Not your team mates, and not in co-op.
- **Bots** (MULTIPLAYER > PLAY WITH BOTS, or bots in a network game) pick up and use the mod's weapons, and kick and finish off other players.
- **Its new enemies,** with the mod's own pictures, sounds and behaviour (read from your copy of the mod):
  - **Skeletal Zombies** and **Crimson Innocents** take the place of about two thirds of the axe zombies, and of some buried ones, which climb out of the ground. A badly hurt Skeletal Zombie jumps on your face and bites: press USE fast to push it off. Crimson Innocents punch and spew blood.
  - **Attack dogs** come with some of the shotgun and dynamite cultists. A dog killed by spirit damage gets back up as a **Monster Dog**.
  - **Necromancers** (instead of a tommy gun cultist for every 75 enemies in a level): they teleport away when you get close, fire skulls, raise corpses and call zombies out of the ground. **Odious Headstones** appear near you every few minutes while one lives, and raise zombies until you blow them up. Some dead Necromancers come back as a **Lich**.
  - **Lesser Priests** (some tommy gun and tesla cultists), **rats with dynamite**, **Slime Zombies**, the **Gore Tremor** (it moves underground and bursts out to bite or spit worms), **Flesh Tentacles**, the **Inferno**, **Spirits** and **worms**.
  - Shoot an axe zombie in the belly and its top half keeps crawling after you; shoot its head off and the head may keep biting.
- **More gore:** head shots, belly shots and leg shots kill with the mod's own animations (half of the cultists shot in the belly lie there suffering), and spirit and tesla kills too.

**Also in this build (with or without the mod):** OPTIONS > GAME SETUP > **WEAPON SWITCH LIST**. When you switch weapons, your weapons show for a moment in a column on the right side, with the new one highlighted. It's off by default.

**Choosing the mod now restarts the app by itself.**

- **Its weapons:** they share the number keys with Blood's; press the key again for the next one on it.
  - **Caleb's revolvers** (key 1) are the mod's pitchfork: twelve shots, then a reload; they never run out. Alt fire: **Purify**, five seconds of them shooting every enemy in sight by themselves, then ten seconds to wait. Crouch + fire: the **Purge**, both barrels' blast (ten seconds to wait). Crouch + alt fire: the **knife**.
  - **Gatling Gun** (key 7): it's where the Guns Akimbo powerup was. Hold fire; alt fire is its wider "overkill".
  - **Devastator** and **rocket launcher** (key 7), and the **Trench Gun** (key 3): pick them up where the mod's maps place them. The rocket launcher's alt fire is the nuke, which takes ten rockets. The Trench Gun's alt fire is dragon's breath.
  - **Devil's Bore** (key 8): it's where Beast Vision was.
  - **Dual tommy guns, dual sawed-offs and dual flare guns:** pick up a second one of the gun you already have.
  - **Mourning Codex** (key 0): it's where Reflective Shots was. One spell, then the book is gone. Fire: thirteen flesh gargoyles fight for you for 15 seconds. Alt fire: a blizzard of ice spikes around you. Crouch + fire: lightning. Crouch + alt fire: Caleb's heart opens a Death Portal that pulls enemies in. (In the mod, the last two are on the map zoom keys.)
  - **Things the dead leave** (pick them up with **USE**, key 1): an axe zombie's **axe** now and then (swing it, or alt fire to throw it; it can be picked up again where it lands), a Phantasm's **orb** (thrown, it finds the head of the enemy you look at and drills through it), and from each Necromancer either the **Vile Anathema** (fire: the corpses you can see rise and fight for you; alt fire: 15 seconds of skulls flying from your shoulder at what you look at) or the **Bastion Chronometer** (fire: 30 seconds of reflective shots; alt fire: the Doom Bell, which rings death all around it and then blows apart). Each is used once.
- **Its world weapons** (USE in front of them, up to about 3 steps away):
  - **Death Mower:** it runs by itself for 37 seconds and shreds what's in front of it. Fire throws it away.
  - **Maxim gun** on its stand: USE gets behind it, USE again leaves it. JUMP rips it off its stand and you carry it (the mod uses RUN, which auto-run holds).
  - **Blunderbuss:** one big blast, then it's thrown away.
  - **Cannons:** one shot, then the cannon is empty. Carry a **cannon ball** to it (USE to pick it up, USE at the cannon to load it).
  - **Fireworks cannon:** a show of rockets, then it's thrown away.
  - The **AA guns** fire flak into the sky when you're near.
- **Allies** (the Codex's gargoyles, the risen dead) don't attack you, and autoaim skips them. They don't count as kills.

**Not included (yet):** the Hero/Slasher powerups (Ash, Draven, Candyman, Leatherface, the Terminator, Krueger, Voorhees), Wrath Demon Mode, the Sword, the mod's changes to Blood's own weapons, and flying the Phantasm Orb by hand (its alt fire throws it like fire does).

To play the normal game again: MODS > ORIGINAL GAME.

Nothing of the mod is in this download. It only reads the mod files you put in `mods/BEC_1.2`.
