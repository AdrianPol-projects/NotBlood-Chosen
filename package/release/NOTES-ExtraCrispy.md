# Blood Extra Crispy in NotBlood Chosen (Android test build)

A test of the *Blood Extra Crispy* mod (by VGames, for Nightdive's Refreshed Supply) as a mod for NotBlood Chosen. The normal release is [v1.2](https://github.com/AdrianPol-projects/NotBlood-Chosen/releases/latest); this build is only for trying the mod.

**The app:** it's called *NotBlood Crispy* and installs next to NotBlood instead of replacing it. Both use the same `NotBlood` folder on the phone, so your Blood files, settings and saves are shared.

**Setup:**
1. Install the APK. If you have never used NotBlood on this phone, copy your Blood files into the `NotBlood` folder first (see the [v1.2 notes](https://github.com/AdrianPol-projects/NotBlood-Chosen/releases/latest)).
2. Download **Blood Extra Crispy v1.2** from ModDB (`BEC_v1.2_RS.zip`) and unpack it.
3. Copy its `BEC_1.2` folder, unchanged, into `NotBlood/mods/` on the phone. The result is `NotBlood/mods/BEC_1.2/EXTRAC.RFF` and so on.
4. Start NotBlood Crispy, go to **MODS**, choose **BLOOD EXTRA CRISPY**. The game restarts with the mod.

**What you get:**
- **Its look:** the title and menu background, and its edited sprites.
- **Its sounds.**
- **Its maps:** the mod's versions of the official levels, with its extra decorations, gore and enemies.
- **Gory Kills:** walk up to a nearly dead enemy, face it and press **USE**. Caleb finishes it in first person, heals a little, and kicks the body away. They work on cultists (all four), axe zombies, fat zombies, flesh gargoyles, gill beasts, innocents, hell hounds and phantasms. The enemy must be badly hurt (below about 12 health), except innocents. Single player only.
- **Quick kick:** USE on an enemy that isn't hurt enough shows Caleb's foot and kicks it back. Burning enemies and beasts always get kicked. You can kick once a second.
- **Its new enemies,** with the mod's own pictures, sounds and behaviour (read from your copy of the mod):
  - **Skeletal Zombies** and **Crimson Innocents** take the place of about two thirds of the axe zombies, and of some buried ones, which climb out of the ground. A badly hurt Skeletal Zombie jumps on your face and bites: press USE fast to push it off. Crimson Innocents punch and spew blood.
  - **Attack dogs** come with some of the shotgun and dynamite cultists. A dog killed by spirit damage gets back up as a **Monster Dog**.
  - **Necromancers** (instead of a tommy gun cultist for every 75 enemies in a level): they teleport away when you get close, fire skulls, raise corpses and call zombies out of the ground. **Odious Headstones** appear near you every few minutes while one lives, and raise zombies until you blow them up. Some dead Necromancers come back as a **Lich**.
  - **Lesser Priests** (some tommy gun and tesla cultists), **rats with dynamite**, **Slime Zombies**, the **Gore Tremor** (it moves underground and bursts out to bite or spit worms), **Flesh Tentacles**, the **Inferno**, **Spirits** and **worms**.
  - Shoot an axe zombie in the belly and its top half keeps crawling after you; shoot its head off and the head may keep biting.
- **More gore:** head shots, belly shots and leg shots kill with the mod's own animations (half of the cultists shot in the belly lie there suffering), and spirit and tesla kills too.

**Also in this build (with or without the mod):** OPTIONS > GAME SETUP > **WEAPON SWITCH LIST**. When you switch weapons, your weapons show for a moment in a column on the right side, with the new one highlighted. It's off by default.

**Choosing the mod now restarts the app by itself.**

**Not included (yet):** the mod's new weapons (the Gatling Gun, Devil's Bore, Trench Gun, dual guns and the rest) and its scripted extras (turrets, lawn mowers, cannons). Their pictures can show in the maps as plain decorations.

To play the normal game again: MODS > ORIGINAL GAME.

Nothing of the mod is in this download. It only reads the mod files you put in `mods/BEC_1.2`.
